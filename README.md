# RegWave

**Regulatory intelligence for Anti-Financial Crime and financial-services compliance teams.**

RegWave helps compliance teams monitor regulatory developments, identify what is relevant to their institution, review AI-assisted suggestions, and preserve a clear human decision trail.

> **AI suggests. Your team decides.**

---

## 🧭 Overview

RegWave automates the collection, AI-assisted processing, and structured review of regulatory developments and enforcement actions from major U.S. financial regulatory and enforcement sources.

It monitors developments relevant to:

- **BSA** — Bank Secrecy Act
- **AML** — Anti-Money Laundering
- **KYC/CDD** — Know Your Customer / Customer Due Diligence
- **Sanctions/OFAC**
- **ABC** — Anti-Bribery & Corruption
- **Fraud** and financial-crime enforcement
- **Cybersecurity** and related supervisory expectations
- **Consumer protection**
- Broader regulatory and supervisory developments affecting financial institutions

The workflow is simple:

**Monitor → Organize → Suggest → Human Review → Share / Act → Audit**

Instead of relying on regulator websites, inboxes, spreadsheets, and informal notes, teams work from one review environment that is refreshed every day.

## 🏛️ Regulatory Sources

Coverage includes regulatory updates, enforcement actions, speeches, guidance, announcements, and other developments relevant to U.S. financial-services compliance.

New publications are collected once a day.

Not every publication type from every source is covered.

## 🎯 Institution-Specific Relevance

RegWave does more than collect regulatory news.

Each team works with its own relevance profile, so the same regulatory development can be evaluated through that institution's specific compliance lens.

A relevance profile can reflect factors such as:

- institution type
- products and services
- customer base
- geographic exposure
- regulatory responsibilities
- financial-crime risks
- internal compliance priorities

RegWave evaluates new items against that profile and provides an **AI relevance suggestion with a short reason**.

Reviewers then make the final decision:

- **Relevant**
- **Irrelevant**
- **Review Later**

The AI suggestion never becomes the institution's decision automatically.

## 🧠 AI-Assisted Classification

Each new item is assigned exactly one category:

- **BSA/AML/KYC Fines & Enforcement**
- **Regulatory Updates**
- **Regulator Speeches & Testimony**
- **Industry News & Trends**

Each item can also receive up to three AFC topic tags:

- AML/BSA
- KYC/CDD
- Sanctions/OFAC
- Fraud
- Bribery/Corruption
- SAR/Reporting
- Transaction Monitoring
- Beneficial Ownership
- Cybersecurity
- Consumer Protection
- Capital/Prudential

RegWave also produces a two-sentence plain-English summary of each item: what happened, and why it matters to a compliance team.

## 👥 Shared Corpus, Private Team Review

RegWave uses a **shared corpus, private review** model.

The underlying regulatory publications are shared across the platform, while each team's work remains isolated.

Private team data includes:

- triage decisions
- notes
- category corrections
- relevance suggestions
- alert preferences
- sharing history
- audit history

Two teams can review the same publication independently without seeing each other's decisions or notes.

Access is invitation-based and role-aware.

---

## 📥 Unified Review Queue

All monitored sources feed one review Queue.

Reviewers can:

- filter by regulator
- filter by category
- filter by review status
- filter by date
- filter by shared status
- search across the corpus
- review AI relevance suggestions
- record triage decisions
- add notes
- reclassify categories (the original AI category is preserved)
- identify related publications
- track whether an item has already been shared

Triage decisions are confirmed before they are recorded, and simultaneous edits by teammates are caught rather than overwritten.

The goal is to turn regulatory monitoring into a controlled review process rather than a stream of disconnected alerts.

## 🔎 Smart Search & Exact Search

RegWave supports two complementary search modes.

### Smart Search

Smart Search combines meaning-based retrieval with keyword matching so users can find conceptually related items without losing obvious exact matches.

Examples:

- `money laundering`
- `beneficial ownership`
- `sanctions evasion`
- `fraud controls`
- `customer due diligence`

### Exact Search

Exact mode is designed for literal searches such as:

- agency names
- rule numbers
- company names
- specific legal terms
- exact phrases

Search works together with active Queue filters.

## 🔁 Duplicate Detection

The same regulatory action is often published by multiple agencies, and sometimes republished by the same agency.

RegWave detects likely duplicates and links related publications so reviewers can recognize when several items describe the same underlying event.

Duplicates are **flagged, not merged**.

Every publication remains independently visible and keeps its own:

- source
- triage decision
- notes
- category
- audit history

An administrator can dismiss an incorrect duplicate link.

This reduces repetitive review without hiding source material or automatically copying decisions from one publication to another.

---

## 🔔 Alerts

Users can set up email digests based on:

- frequency (daily or weekly)
- regulators
- compliance themes

Themes are matched by meaning, not only by exact keyword, so digests can be tailored to the subjects that matter to a particular reviewer or team.

A daily digest covers the previous day; a weekly digest covers the previous seven days. No email is sent when nothing matches.

---

## ✉️ Sharing & Distribution

RegWave supports several ways to distribute reviewed intelligence by email.

### Share Filtered

A reviewer can send the current filtered set of articles to a stakeholder, with an optional note.

Successful shares are recorded so the team can see which items have already been distributed.

### Per-Article Share

Individual articles can be forwarded directly from the review interface.

### Share Tracking

The Queue shows whether articles have already been shared, helping teams distinguish items already distributed from items still awaiting action.

Share history records when an item was shared and by whom. Recipient email addresses are not stored.

## 🧾 Audit Trail

RegWave records meaningful review activity so a team can reconstruct what happened later.

The audit record can capture:

- triage decisions
- decision changes
- notes
- category corrections
- shares

Entries include actor and timestamp information.

Audit entries cannot be edited or deleted by any user, including administrators. The audit trail is visible to team administrators, not to all members.

The purpose is not only to know the team's current position, but to be able to explain:

- what was reviewed
- what decision was made
- who made it
- when it happened
- what changed later

## 🛡️ Security & Data Handling

RegWave is designed around several basic principles:

- authenticated access
- invitation-based onboarding
- role-based permissions
- team-level data separation
- server-side handling of privileged credentials
- encrypted network transport
- controlled access to private team records
- isolated public demo environment
- auditable human review activity

AI features rely on third-party model providers. Those providers process public regulatory text and, where a feature needs it, a team's relevance profile or a user's alert themes.

RegWave does not publish unnecessary implementation details or claim security certifications it has not earned.

More detailed security and data-handling information can be provided during customer or procurement review.

---

## 👤 Human-in-the-Loop by Design

RegWave is intentionally not an autonomous compliance decision-maker.

AI can:

- classify
- summarize
- suggest relevance
- help retrieve related material
- help identify duplicate publications

AI does **not** make the institution's compliance decision.

A person reviews the item and records the final outcome.
